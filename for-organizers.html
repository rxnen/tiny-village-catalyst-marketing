Questions organizers ask

Is it free?

Yes. The tools are free and openly licensed for community and nonprofit use.

Do we need technical skills?

No. If you can send a text message, you can use it.

Is it only for the Bay Area?

Our experience is in Alameda and Contra Costa counties today, with our live prototype supporting only four Bay Area cities. We expect our platform to grow as communities in new places join.

Can it replace a lawyer or architect?

No. It helps you get to the right questions faster and tells you when you need a professional.

What data do you collect?

Only community- and project-level information. We never collect data about individual residents.
